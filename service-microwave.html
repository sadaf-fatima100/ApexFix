Common Microwave Issues We Resolve On-Site

Our fully-equipped mobile service vans carry digital analyzers and genuine replacement components for all high-voltage microwave failures.

01

Running But Not Heating

Burnt-out magnetron cathode vacuum tubes, shorted high-voltage diodes, open-circuit high-voltage capacitors, and transformer faults.

02

Sparking & Arcing Inside

Carbonized mica waveguide covers, chipped interior cavity enamel, burnt stirrer fan blades, and electrical flashover remediation.

03

Turntable Not Rotating

Stripped 220V synchronous turntable drive motors, broken plastic roller support rings, center coupler fractures, and drive belt replacements.

04
