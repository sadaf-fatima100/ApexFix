Common Dishwasher Issues We Resolve On-Site

Our fully-equipped mobile service vans carry digital analyzers and genuine replacement components for all wash and leak failures.

01

Dishes Not Cleaning / Low Spray

Clogged stainless spray arm jets, wash circulation impeller motor wear, water inlet solenoid valve filters, and microfilter descaling.

02

Water Leak / AquaStop E15

Base pan flood sensor trips, sump bowl silicone seal degradation, door perimeter rubber gasket leaks, and pressurized hose triage.

03

Not Draining / Standing Water

Drain motor pump impellers, non-return check valve flap blockage, sink drain adapter obstructions, and optical water level sensors.

04

No Heat / Wet Dishes at End

Flow-through heat pump element burnouts, NTC temperature thermistor drift, circuit board heater relays, and zeolite drying fan checks.
